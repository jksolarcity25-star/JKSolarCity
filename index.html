🎯 Our approach

Every roof is different. So is every electricity bill.

Orientation, shade, roof material, sanctioned load, and how the building is actually used through the day all change what a good solar system looks like. We don't sell a fixed panel count - we start on-site, measure how your property runs, and design around that.

  • Site assessment first. We check your roof orientation, shading, structure and existing load before we ever talk numbers.
  • System design around real usage. A home that runs air-conditioners all afternoon needs a different design than an office that shuts by 7pm.
  • Our own team on the roof. Installation is supervised directly by JK Solar City, start to finish - not handed off to a subcontractor.
  • Care that continues. Commissioning, monitoring support and lifecycle maintenance are part of the package, not an add-on.

❓ Common questions

What people usually ask before saying yes.

Does solar still work on cloudy or monsoon days?
Yes, though output drops. Panels still generate power from diffused daylight, just less of it than on a clear day. Chennai gets strong sun for most of the year, which is what the "possible starting range" figures above already account for.
Is my roof suitable? What about RCC vs. sheet roofs?
Most RCC terraces and metal sheet roofs can both take solar, with different mounting structures. Shade from trees or neighbouring buildings matters more than roof material. That's exactly what the on-site assessment checks before we design anything.
How long does installation actually take?
A typical home system is usually mounted and wired within a few days on-site, once materials are ready. Larger apartment or commercial jobs take longer depending on system size and any structural work needed. We'll give you a firm timeline after the site assessment.
How does the government subsidy actually work?
Residential rooftop solar can qualify for a total government subsidy of up to ₹1,00,000, including ₹78,000 from the central government and ₹22,000 from the state government, depending on system size and location. We help you with the paperwork and application as part of the process, so you're not chasing it alone.
What happens after installation - is there any maintenance?
Panels need occasional cleaning and a periodic check of connections and inverter health. Our AMC (available up to 5 years) covers this, plus monitoring support, so small issues get caught before they cost you generation.
